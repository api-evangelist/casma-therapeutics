# Casma Therapeutics

> Clinical-stage biotechnology company founded in 2018 and headquartered in Boston, MA, developing cellular degradation medicines built on the autophagy pathway. Its PHLYT (Phagosome-Lysosome Targeting) platform initiates target-specific autophagic degradation of disease targets independent of size or complexity — organelles, protein aggregates and large signaling complexes — across oncology, inflammation, neurodegeneration and metabolic disease.
