In their recent Nature paper, Casma Founders Jim Hurley and Andrea Ballabio describe the cryogenic-EM structure of an MTORC1 megacomplex. A molecular description of this massive complex is helping us understand the regulation of the TFEB transcription factor and dependence of TFEB phosphorylation on FLCN and the RagC GDP state.
